About the Academy

A tutoring programme built on care and results.

Ignite Potential Academy is an online tutoring programme founded by Laurence Chivambo, a medical student at Sefako Makgatho Health Sciences University. We provide affordable, high-quality academic support designed to help students unlock their full potential.

i.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, high-quality online education and mentorship that empowers students to achieve academic success and personal growth.

ii.

Our Vision

To be a leading academy that inspires learners to unlock their full potential and confidently pursue future opportunities.
